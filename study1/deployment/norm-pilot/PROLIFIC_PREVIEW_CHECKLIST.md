# Prolific Preview checklist

- Confirm the Draft Study ID exactly matches `PROLIFIC_EXPECTED_STUDY_ID`.
- Confirm the completion URL exactly matches `PROLIFIC_COMPLETION_URL`.
- Import the frozen private Taskflow CSV without editing its URLs or variants.
- Open one Taskflow entry from each of the four cells in Prolific Preview.
- Confirm PID, Study ID, and Session ID runtime placeholders arrive at the server.
- Complete at least one honest and one pro-misreport flow.
- Confirm completion redirects to the Draft completion destination.
- Confirm Preview records appear only in Preview Admin/export.
- Confirm Formal Admin/export remains empty before recruitment opens.
- Recheck `/health`, then enable Formal recruitment as the final configuration-only action.
